## Payment Advice

AIT tuition and registration fees are charged in Thai Baht, so all students are encouraged to pay in Thai Baht.

For the January 2026 Semester, the AIT Official Rate of Exchange (ROE) of conversion is as follows:

If the invoice is in THB to USD, use the ROE is 28.88If the invoice is in USD to THB, use the ROE is 32.16

If the invoice is in THB to EUR, use the ROE is 33.765If the invoice is in EUR to THB, use the ROE is 37.0475

If the invoice is in THB to GBP, use the ROE is 38.62875If the invoice is in GBP to THB, use the ROE is 42.10375

If the invoice is in THB to AUD, use the ROE is 17.5175If the invoice is in AUD to THB, use the ROE is 20.705
