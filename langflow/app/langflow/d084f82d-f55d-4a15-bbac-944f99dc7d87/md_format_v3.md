## Eligibility

* Country: Pakistan
* Will study Master’s degree program in the field of Structural Engineering
* Graduate of Bachelor with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.75
* Official English Score: IELTS writing score of 5.0 orAIT EETScore of 5.5
* Must fulfill theAIT Admissions Requirements.
